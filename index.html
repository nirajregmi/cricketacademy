Who We Are

About Sagarmatha Cricket Academy

Located in the heart of Bafal-13, Kathmandu, Sagarmatha Cricket Academy is dedicated to nurturing cricketing talent from grassroots level to professional standards. Our academy blends traditional coaching values with modern sports science to develop complete cricketers — technically sound, mentally strong, and physically fit.

We welcome players of all ages and skill levels, offering structured training programs, state-of-the-art nets, and a pathway to district, provincial, and national level cricket.

  • ✔ Certified & experienced coaching staff
  • ✔ Modern turf nets & bowling machines
  • ✔ Age-group specific training programs
  • ✔ Fitness, nutrition & mental conditioning support

What We Offer

Our Training Programs

🧒

Junior Cricket (U-10 / U-14)

Fun-based fundamentals — batting, bowling, fielding basics and love for the game for young beginners.

🎯

Youth Development (U-16 / U-19)

Advanced technique, match strategy and physical conditioning for competitive age-group cricket.

🏆

Elite Performance Program

High-intensity training for aspiring national/provincial players with video analysis and personalized plans.

👩

Women's Cricket Program

Dedicated coaching batches for girls and women focused on skill-building and competitive readiness.

🌙

Weekend & Holiday Camps

Short-term intensive camps during school holidays for skill sharpening and match exposure.

🥎

Individual Coaching

One-on-one personalized sessions with senior coaches for focused, rapid skill improvement.
